ROUND 1 Foundations — tokens, type, component vocabulary

That's So Econ, rebuilt as a place you build something.

The app teaches economics through the arc of a venture, so the interface takes that arc literally: every learner works a plot of land. A finished day puts up a building, a finished chapter lights a block, a finished track leaves a skyline. Progress stops being a number that changes and becomes a place you can look at.

Around that, four rooms with four temperaments. The dashboard is dense and administrative. The roadmap is spatial and lit. Reading is warm paper, one column, nothing else. The quiz is a stage: high contrast, one question, no chrome. Same tokens throughout, different air.

04

The metaphor — one plot, four stages

SVG isometry, gradient faces, one long shadow per volume. This is the roadmap node and the reward moment.
Locked
Empty lot
A day you haven't unlocked. Ground only, no volume.
Day cleared
Corner shop
One quiz passed at 80%. The volume rises and the windows light.
Chapter closed
Works & yard
Seven days plus the review quiz. The whole block gets its lamp.
Track complete
Headquarters
56 days. The building stays on your profile and the leaderboard row.
Cost. Four polygon sets and two gradients per volume. No 3D library, no images, no per-node canvas.
Motion. A cleared day animates one thing: the volume rising 14px with its shadow spreading. 420ms, spring, once.
Fallback. Under prefers-reduced-motion the building is simply already there.
