QUESTION 3 OF 10 ⚡ Medium

Airbnb's hosts could add a guest for almost nothing. Why did that let them price against hotels rather than under them?

A Because with marginal cost near zero, the price is set by what the night is worth to the guest
B Because hotels in those cities had already cut their rates
C Because a spare room costs the same to run as a hotel room
D Because the conference organisers capped local prices
WHY A IS RIGHT

When the cost of serving one more customer approaches zero, cost stops informing price. What remains is willingness to pay — which is why the same bed can be worth $60 in February and $300 during a conference.
