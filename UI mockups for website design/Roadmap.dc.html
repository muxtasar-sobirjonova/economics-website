WHAT CHANGED

The hardcoded x-coordinates go. Plots sit on an isometric grid, so a chapter is generated from its day list — 7 nodes or 70. Completed days carry a volume, the active day is a foundation with dashed intent, locked days are bare ground.

MOBILE NAV

Eight scrolling tabs become four fixed ones — Home, Plot, Learn, League. Learn opens the day sheet in 2c right, where Concept / Article / Quiz live in reading order, with Notes and Mistakes at its foot.
